Our Story

The owner still checks every dish before it leaves the kitchen.

That is not something you find very often. But at Pizza Plus, it has always been that way. Our owner has been welcoming guests personally for years. He knows the regulars. He remembers the first-timers.

The spices are imported directly from India. The curries are prepared the way his family has always prepared them. And the result is the kind of food that people drive back to Kasane for.
